Add the built-in conversation Source: a trusted local kind with no configuration, a task-thread Trigger, and Channel delivery into the main Session; the gate records its user's explicit request with the `local` basis. `orchestrator_dispatch` turns the person's latest message into such a Trigger and accepts it with a direct Eval, so a conversation request becomes one Ticket without a model assessment and without bypassing Eval. A manifest may also make no health claim, for a Source whose only dependency is the runtime.
