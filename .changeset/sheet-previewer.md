Add the sheet previewer: CSV and `.xlsx` workbooks are read as sparse cells and compared by row and column. A formula change is reported as `formula` even when its cached value moved with it, and a cached value under the same formula is reported as `value`, so a formula edit is never counted as a numeric edit. Inserted or removed rows and columns are listed as structure instead of being spread across later cells when unchanged rows or columns anchor the alignment, and unique exact rows keep that true even when thousands of other rows are edited. Wide column runs are matched from a bounded row sample so a moved column is not reported as every cell changing, shared formulas are expanded for their followers, and formulas are compared by cell-relative references so a copied formula shifted by an inserted row is not a formula edit. Unique exact rows anchor the alignment, so a heavily edited table keeps a row insertion structural. A workbook that cannot be parsed degrades to a generic line diff with the original link and the coded parse failure (D4); a readable side contributes its cells, not its ZIP bytes. The previewer registry that stores the preview and gives the Deliverable its `PreviewRef` comes with the review surface.
