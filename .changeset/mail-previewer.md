Add the mail previewer: two structured mail drafts are compared into recipient, subject, body and attachment sections. The recipient envelope is compared per address, so a move between To, Cc and Bcc — including the dangerous move to Bcc — is one explicit `changed` recipient rather than a removal and a hidden addition. `recipients.audience` lists every address the after revision will reach, once, with its fields and Bcc visibility, and coded warnings name a Bcc presence, a move to Bcc, an address in more than one field and an empty envelope. A joined multi-address string and a subject with a line break are refused as caller errors rather than read as one recipient or a header. The body is a line diff of the readable text that trims the common prefix and suffix before aligning, and `body.alignment` marks an oversized middle that paired by position. An attachment with content or a digest on both sides is compared by that fingerprint, a one-sided fingerprint is a content change, and `attachments.unverified` names pairs whose content had only metadata compared. The previewer registry that stores the preview and gives the Deliverable its `PreviewRef` comes with the review surface.
